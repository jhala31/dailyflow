Privacy Policy for DailyFlow

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

DailyFlow ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DailyFlow mobile and web application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

DailyFlow does not require account registration and does not collect, transmit, or store your personal financial or habit data on external servers. All expenses, habits, and preferences you enter are stored locally on your own device using your browser/app local storage.

2. Third-Party Services

DailyFlow does not sell or share your data with third parties. If future updates introduce optional in-app purchases or analytics, this policy will be updated accordingly before those features are enabled.

3. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

DailyFlow is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
